## 1. Executive Summary & Sprint Mission

Following the completion and forensic verification of the Sagar-Drishti V2.3 Model D shadow inference pipeline, this engineering sprint established the complete infrastructure to safely observe Model D over time, accumulate independent empirical evidence, compare it continuously against production v1.1.0, detect data-quality problems and atmospheric feature drift, and expose secure research-only analytics.

The system addresses the 12 core operational questions:
1. **Valid Input Verification**: Validates the complete 224-feature vector (101 ocean + 123 atmospheric) without NaNs/Infs.
2. **Shadow Coverage Tracking**: Aggregates opportunities, submissions, executions, and drops.
3. **Atmospheric Data Latency**: Tracks missing or delayed ERA5 reanalysis fields relative to the T 18:00 UTC causal cutoff.
4. **Failure Auditing**: Categorizes failures across schemas, timestamps, missing feeds, and inference exceptions.
5. **Queue Saturation Monitoring**: Observes bounded queue depth and logs dropped jobs without blocking production.
6. **Production vs Shadow Divergence**: Computes descriptive statistics (means, medians, deltas, percentiles, correlation) per horizon.
7. **Temporal Evolution**: Tracks probability series across sequential observations.
8. **Storm System Tracking**: Accumulates evidence across distinct cyclonic disturbances in the North Indian Ocean.
9. **Independent Event Accounting**: Strictly isolates future post-2026 events from the quarantined 2025–2026 test split.
10. **Horizon Evidence Depth**: Tracks sample size readiness across each lead time (0d, 1d, 2d, 3d).
11. **Scientific Audit Readiness**: Evaluates evidence maturity against transparent thresholds.
12. **Distribution Shift & Drift Detection**: Computes Kolmogorov-Smirnov and Population Stability Index (PSI) against baseline distributions.

### 3.2 Deduplication Invariant
Because production requests may be queried repeatedly for identical timestamps, sites, and horizons, every telemetry line is mapped to a deterministic `observation_id`. Re-ingesting a telemetry file or concurrent worker ingestion executes `INSERT OR IGNORE`, preventing artificial inflation of evaluation sample sizes.

## 4. Evidence Maturity & Scientific Safeguards

### 4.1 Evidence Maturity Scale

The accumulation layer enforces a 5-tier maturity progression:

```
[INSUFFICIENT] ──> [EARLY] ──> [DEVELOPING] ──> [SUBSTANTIAL] ──> [PROMOTION_REVIEW_ELIGIBLE]
```

1. **`INSUFFICIENT`**:
   - Criteria: $< 3$ independent future storm systems OR $< 25$ positive shadow observations.
   - Status: System forbids conclusive performance claims. Statistical evaluation returns `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`.
2. **`EARLY`**:
   - Criteria: 3–4 independent future storm systems observed. Preliminary signal accumulation begins.
3. **`DEVELOPING`**:
   - Criteria: 5–9 independent future storm systems across $\ge 1$ active storm season.
4. **`SUBSTANTIAL`**:
   - Criteria: $\ge 10$ independent storm systems across $\ge 2$ distinct monsoon seasons.
5. **`PROMOTION_REVIEW_ELIGIBLE`**:
   - Criteria: $\ge 15$ independent future storm systems, $\ge 2$ full calendar years, and $\ge 95\%$ shadow data quality coverage.
   - **Critical Guardrail**: *Promotion Review Eligible does NOT mean "Promote Model D"*. It exclusively authorizes the convening of an independent scientific and operational review committee.

### 4.2 Statistical Significance Safeguard
To prevent misleading statistical claims from small sample sizes:
- If positive observations $< 50$ or unique storm systems $< 5$:
  - `status = "INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE"`
  - `statistical_significance_claim_allowed = False`
  - Any calculated contingency metrics (Precision, Recall, F1, CSI, FAR, Brier score) are provisional.

## 5. Data Quality, Freshness & Feature Drift Monitoring

### 5.1 Atmospheric Feature Drift Detection
The monitoring layer continually compares shadow-period atmospheric distributions against the 10-year training baseline (`backend/data/era5/features_atmosphere_10yr_daily.parquet`).

Evaluated features:
- `max_vorticity`
- `mean_vorticity`
- `min_vws`
- `mean_rh700`
- `p90_vws`

Statistical Tests:
1. **Two-Sample Kolmogorov-Smirnov Test**: Calculates distance statistic $D$ and asymptotic $p$-value.
2. **Population Stability Index (PSI)**: Evaluates shift across 10 quantile bins:
   $$\text{PSI} = \sum_{i=1}^{10} (T_i - R_i) \times \ln\left(\frac{T_i}{R_i}\right)$$
3. **Thresholds**:
   - If $\text{PSI} > 0.25$ or ($D > 0.15$ and $p < 0.001$):
     - Emits `DATA_QUALITY_WARNING` / `RESEARCH_WARNING`.
     - **Strict Isolation**: A drift warning is an internal research notice and NEVER creates an operational alert.

### 5.2 Atmospheric Freshness & NWP Transition
- Evaluates the operational delay: $\Delta t = t_{\text{prediction}} - t_{\text{atmosphere}}$.
- Reanalysis data latency from ERA5 differs significantly from real-time operational NWP model cycles (e.g. IMD GFS / NCUM). Tracking freshness statistics establishes empirical latency requirements for the future NWP migration phase.

## 7. Operationalization & Future NWP Migration Considerations

When Sagar-Drishti eventually transitions from historical ERA5 reanalysis to live operational NWP feeds:
1. **NWP Latency Boundaries**: Operational NWP forecasts (00Z, 06Z, 12Z, 18Z cycles) arrive with distinct run delays. The causal cutoff logic must ingest forecast cycles available at run time.
2. **Spatial Interpolation**: Global NWP grids (e.g. $0.125^\circ$ or $0.25^\circ$) must match the spatial bounding boxes (`NBOB`, `CBOB`, `SBOB`, `NAS`, `CAS`, `SAS`) without grid misalignment.
3. **Forecast Lead Time Alignment**: The 0d, 1d, 2d, and 3d lead horizons will ingest corresponding NWP forecast steps ($T+0\text{h}$, $T+24\text{h}$, $T+48\text{h}$, $T+72\text{h}$).
4. **Pre-Operational NWP Shadow Phase**: A dedicated shadow observation phase (V2.4) using live NWP must precede any operational promotion review.
