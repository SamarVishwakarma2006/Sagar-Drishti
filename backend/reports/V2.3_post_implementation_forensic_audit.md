## F. Bootstrap Methodology & Temporal Clustering Audit

### Audit of the 2,000 Paired Bootstrap Procedure
1. **Resampling Unit:** Individual rows ($N = 1,074$).
2. **Pairing:** Strictly maintained (`boot_idx` applied identically to both models).
3. **Delta Calculation:** $\Delta = \text{Metric}(\text{Model D}) - \text{Metric}(\text{Model A})$ per replicate.

## G. Statistical Caveats & Limitations

1. **Multiplicity Caveat:** The experiment evaluated 5 architectures, 4 horizons, and multiple metrics without a formal family-wise error rate (FWER) correction. When applying a conservative Bonferroni correction for the 4 primary lead horizons ($\alpha_{\text{adjusted}} = 0.05 / 4 = 0.0125$), 1d and 2d leads survive under both row-wise and block bootstrap, while 3d lead ($p = 0.0186$ block bootstrap) is nominally significant at $\alpha = 0.05$.
2. **$p$-Value Reporting:** Literal $p = 0.000$ has been corrected to $p < 0.0005$ throughout all reports.
3. **Sample Size:** The test set contained 21 positive daily observation rows across 2 storm systems. While sufficient to establish nominal significance under non-parametric bootstrap, it represents a limited sample of meteorological regimes.
