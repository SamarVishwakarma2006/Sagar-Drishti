### 3.2 Evaluation of Candidate Spatial Mapping Strategies

`[PROPOSED EXPERIMENT]` Four spatial mapping strategies were evaluated scientifically:

#### Strategy 1: Multi-Sub-basin Hierarchical Prefix Flattening (`RECOMMENDED`)
* **Mechanism:** For each ocean observation at `site_id='bob'` on date $T$, attach the features of all 3 constituent Bay of Bengal sub-basins (`nbob_*`, `cbob_*`, `sbob_*`) as prefixed horizontal columns. For `site_id='aras'`, attach the 3 Arabian Sea sub-basins (`nas_*`, `cas_*`, `sas_*`).
* **Row Multiplier:** Exactly 1:1. Ocean row count is preserved at exactly **7,246 rows**.
* **Feature Count:** $101 \text{ (Ocean)} + 3 \times 41 \text{ (ERA5)} = 224 \text{ features}$.
* **Geographic Rationale:** Cyclogenesis is non-local: a depression frequently initiates in the South Bay of Bengal (`SBOB`), intensifies as it tracks northwestward through `CBOB`, and makes landfall in `NBOB`. Collapsing these basins into a single mean destroys spatial steering gradients (e.g. upper-level easterly jet shear between SBOB and NBOB). Flattening preserves the spatial atmospheric gradient across the entire basin.
* **Leakage Risk:** Zero. Atmospheric features at date $T$ for sub-basins in the Bay of Bengal are co-temporal with ocean features in the Bay of Bengal.
* **Information Loss:** Zero. All sub-basin local extrema and vorticity centers are retained.

#### Strategy 2: Single Dominant Co-located Sub-basin Mapping
* **Mechanism:** Map `bob` strictly to `CBOB` (maximum centroid overlap) and `aras` strictly to `CAS`.
* **Row Multiplier:** 1:1 (7,246 rows).
* **Feature Count:** $101 + 41 = 142 \text{ features}$.
* **Fatal Flaw:** Blind to genesis in Southern basins (`SBOB`, `SAS`) and landfalling intensification in Northern basins (`NBOB`, `NAS`). Introduces significant scientific false negatives.

#### Strategy 3: Area-Weighted Spatial Pooling
* **Mechanism:** Average ERA5 gridcells or sub-basin values back into the exact ocean bounding box coordinates.
* **Fatal Flaw:** Relative vorticity ($\zeta_{850}$) and vertical wind shear are point-vortex and shear-band phenomena. Spatial averaging across an $8^\circ \times 10^\circ$ box dilutes extreme vorticity peaks ($\zeta > 5 \times 10^{-5} \text{ s}^{-1}$) down into ambient background noise, erasing the primary physical signal of incipient cyclogenesis.

#### Strategy 4: Full All-6 Basins Global Joint Feature Vector
* **Mechanism:** Attach all 6 basins (`nas_*`, `cas_*`, `sas_*`, `nbob_*`, `cbob_*`, `sbob_*`) to every ocean row.
* **Fatal Flaw:** Adds 246 atmospheric features ($6 \times 41$), creating severe cross-basin noise (e.g. Arabian Sea shear features directly influencing Bay of Bengal predictions) and increasing risk of spurious overfitting.

### 3.3 Strategy Decision & Recommendation
`[PROPOSED EXPERIMENT]` **Strategy 1 (Hierarchical Regional Prefix Flattening)** is selected as the only scientifically defensible mapping. It completely avoids Cartesian explosions, strictly maintains the 7,246 row integrity, and preserves meteorological spatial gradients.

## 8. Probabilistic Evaluation Metrics Framework

### 8.1 Primary Evaluation Metrics
`[PROPOSED EXPERIMENT]` Because cyclone occurrence is an extreme-imbalance probabilistic risk event ($\approx 4\%$ positive class rate), accuracy and simple ROC-AUC are insufficient. The evaluation suite must prioritize:

1. **PR-AUC (Precision-Recall Area Under Curve):** Primary metric. Accurately reflects false positive penalties in low base-rate regimes.
2. **Brier Score ($BS$) & Brier Skill Score ($BSS$):** Measures probabilistic forecast calibration and sharpness:
   $$BS = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^N (p_i - y_i)^2, \quad BSS = 1 - \frac{BS}{BS_{\text{climatology}}}$$
3. **Expected Calibration Error (ECE):** Evaluates whether a predicted 70% risk actually corresponds to a 70% empirical event frequency.
4. **Recall at Fixed Operational Alert Burden ($\le 5\%$ Alert Budget):** Measures the proportion of genuine cyclones detected when the system is constrained to issue alerts on no more than 5% of all operational days.
5. **ROC-AUC:** Secondary metric for global ranking discrimination across all thresholds.

### 8.2 The Scientific Decision Criterion: Incremental Delta ($\Delta$)
`[PROPOSED EXPERIMENT]` Atmospheric features cannot be promoted simply because an atmosphere-only model performs well. The central decision criterion is **Incremental Predictive Value Beyond Ocean Memory**:

$$\Delta \text{PR-AUC} = \text{PR-AUC}_{\text{Multimodal}} - \text{PR-AUC}_{\text{Ocean-Only Baseline}}$$
$$\Delta \text{BSS} = BSS_{\text{Multimodal}} - BSS_{\text{Ocean-Only Baseline}}$$

#### Minimum Promotion Gates
To justify architectural adoption into V2.3:
* $\Delta \text{PR-AUC} \ge +0.030$ on the Validation set with statistical significance ($p < 0.01$ via 1,000 paired bootstrap iterations).
* $\Delta BSS \ge +0.020$ with no calibration degradation ($\Delta \text{ECE} \le 0.00$).
* No degradation in lead-time recall at Lead-2 (48h) or Lead-3 (72h).

## 10. Known Scientific Limitations & Operational Caveats

1. `[UNRESOLVED ISSUE]` **Spatial Resolution Disparity:** Copernicus ocean reanalysis is resolved at $0.083^\circ$ ($\approx 9\text{ km}$), whereas ERA5 reanalysis is resolved at $0.25^\circ$ ($\approx 28\text{ km}$). Mesoscale atmospheric features under 30 km (e.g. inner-core eye dynamics) are smoothed out in ERA5.
2. `[UNRESOLVED ISSUE]` **Rigid Rectangular Sub-basin Boundaries:** Cyclones frequently traverse sub-basin borders (e.g. crossing $18^\circ\text{N}$ from `CBOB` into `NBOB`). While Strategy 1 provides horizontal visibility into all 3 sub-basins, the model must learn spatial boundary crossings via interaction trees rather than an explicit Lagrangian tracking framework.
3. `[UNRESOLVED ISSUE]` **Operational Ingestion Latency:** ERA5 final reanalysis has a multi-month latency; operational V2.3 deployment will require near-real-time ECMWF IFS or ERA5T streams. Real-time operational latency differences between ocean and atmospheric feeds must be accommodated in backend ingestion pipelines.
