## Executive Summary

We executed the frozen Sagar-Drishti V2.3 Ocean + Atmosphere ablation experiment to determine whether ERA5 atmospheric dynamic and thermodynamic fields provide statistically significant, leak-free, out-of-sample predictive gains over the established 101-feature ocean-only model.

### Key Empirical Findings:
1. **[VERIFIED RESULT]** Atmospheric integration provides substantial, statistically significant predictive ranking and probability sharpness gains across 1d, 2d, and 3d operational horizons.
2. **[EXPERIMENTAL RESULT]** On the quarantined 2025–2026 out-of-sample test set (1,074 rows, 21 positive daily observation rows across 2 storm systems), Model D (**Ocean + All Atmosphere**) achieved a **+181.9% relative increase in PR-AUC** over Model A (Ocean Only) at the 3d lead, jumping from `0.0645` to `0.1818` ($\Delta = +0.1309$, 95% bootstrap CI: `[+0.0378, +0.2629]`, $p < 0.0005$). Under 7-day block bootstrap accounting for temporal autocorrelation, the gain remains statistically significant ($\Delta = +0.1263$, 95% CI: `[+0.0013, +0.3369]`, $p = 0.0186$).
3. **[EXPERIMENTAL RESULT]** True positive detections at 3d lead nearly doubled from **8 to 14** (out of 21 positive daily observation rows across Cyclone `Montha` and Depression `UNNAMED-12`), while false alarms were simultaneously reduced from **203 to 173**, decreasing the False Alarm Rate from `19.28%` to `16.43%`. At the storm level, Model D detected **2 of 2 storm events** (100%), whereas Model A detected **1 of 2 storm events** (50%), completely missing `UNNAMED-12` in the Arabian Sea.
4. **[VERIFIED RESULT]** Probability calibration improved across all 4 lead times: Brier score decreased significantly from `0.0963` to `0.0822` at 3d ($\Delta = -0.0141$, 95% CI: `[-0.0197, -0.0087]`, $p < 0.0005$), with zero horizons exhibiting calibration degradation.
5. **[RECOMMENDATION]** Meets all project criteria for **CASE 1**. Model D is recommended for **SHADOW EVALUATION ONLY**. Production `v1.1.0` and candidate `v2_10yr` remain 100% untouched.

## 7. Research Threshold Sweep Analysis

A systematic threshold sweep across $\theta \in [0.10, 0.50]$ (step $0.01$) revealed:

## 12. Scientific Ablation Findings

Addressing the 7 core project ablation questions:

1. **Does atmosphere add incremental information?**  
   **YES.** PR-AUC increases by $+181.9\%$ at 3d lead, ROC-AUC increases by $+16.5$ points, and true positive daily detections increase from 8 to 14 out of 21 positive daily observations across 2 storm systems.
2. **Which feature groups contribute?**  
   - **Base features (Model B)** provide the foundational physical signal (vorticity, vertical wind shear, and mid-level humidity). Model B alone achieves `0.3844` PR-AUC on 2024 validation and `0.1689` on 2025–2026 test at 3d lead.
   - **Dynamic lookback derivatives (Model C)** provide temporal trend signals (vorticity intensification rate, shear changes).
   - **Full combination (Model D)** achieves the highest overall accuracy and calibration across lead times.
3. **At which horizons?**  
   The largest relative improvements occur at **1d, 2d, and 3d leads**, where ocean thermal inertia alone cannot detect whether warm water will actually organize into a convective cyclone. At 0d (same day active hazard), the ocean signal is already strong, so PR-AUC gains are non-significant ($p = 0.131$), though calibration sharpness is still improved.
4. **Does atmosphere improve calibration?**  
   **YES.** Brier score is reduced across all 4 horizons ($p < 0.0005$), and ECE drops from `0.2043` to `0.1924` at 3d lead.
5. **Does it increase false alarms?**  
   **NO.** False alarms **decreased** from 203 to 173 at 3d lead and from 170 to 89 at 1d lead. This observation is consistent with the meteorological understanding that vertical wind shear inhibits cyclone formation in marginal warm waters, reducing false alerts.
6. **Is improvement robust?**  
   **YES.** Both validation and out-of-sample test splits show consistent improvements. The 95% confidence intervals for PR-AUC and Brier at 1d, 2d, and 3d leads exclude zero under both row-wise and 7-day block bootstrap resampling.
7. **Is improvement practically meaningful?**  
   **YES.** Alerting on 14 positive daily storm observations instead of 8 across 2 storm systems (including capturing 6 active days of the Arabian Sea depression where the ocean-only model was completely blind) while reducing false alarms by $14.8\%$ constitutes a meaningful operational improvement.

## 14. Limitations & Statistical Caveats

1. **[LIMITATION] Event-Level Sample Size:** The test period contained 21 positive daily observation rows spanning 2 unique storm systems. While non-parametric bootstrap and block bootstrap confirm statistical significance, performance under a wider diversity of storm intensities requires longer shadow testing.
2. **[LIMITATION] Temporal Autocorrelation:** Daily rows within storm events are temporally correlated. 7-day block bootstrap accounts for this clustering, widening confidence intervals while preserving nominal significance ($p < 0.05$).
3. **[LIMITATION] Multiple Comparisons:** Multiple architectures, horizons, and metrics were evaluated without a formal prespecified multiplicity adjustment. When applying a conservative Bonferroni correction for the 4 horizons ($\alpha = 0.0125$), 1d and 2d leads remain strictly significant ($p < 0.01$), while 3d lead ($p = 0.0186$) is nominally significant at $\alpha = 0.05$.
4. **[LIMITATION] Reanalysis vs Real-Time NWP Latency:** ERA5 is a reanalysis product with multi-day latency. Operationalizing this candidate will require real-time numerical weather prediction feeds (ECMWF HRES or GFS) mapped to identical physical units.
